Rot. Husk. Loser

Two plays in dialogue reflect an atomised city. Winners. Losers. Jokers. Addictions. Work. Fragility. Backbones.

From writers Simon Castle, Somebody Jones and Nusrath Tapadar, ROT. HUSK. LOSER. is a unique collaboration that brings together two stories about lies, vanity and love.

Backstage at a gig, an up-and-coming pop band struggle under the pressure of rivalries and unearthed secrets. In a women’s prison something is growing that is red, alive and strictly forbidden.

★★★★

Husk is something of a misnomer as this bitesize play is fully formed and packs a punch worth cheering for.
