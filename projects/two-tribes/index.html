Two Tribes

1987, Leeds. Two rival football hooligan firms descend on the city to watch football and fight. But when two opposing fans fall in secret love, they’re forced to answer the question: Can love survive where masculinity thrives?

Shortlisted for the Tony Craze Award, 2022 and The Charlie Hartill Award, 2025, Two Tribes had it’s R&D at Leeds Playhouse and Theatre Royal Stratford East.
