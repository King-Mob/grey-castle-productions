Engagement Producing

Simon designs and delivers bespoke engagement programmes around new writing, be that during an R&D, production or touring.

From paid trainee placements and writer commissions to workshops, open rehearsals and post-show events, each programme is built to strengthen the production itself, developing new artists, identifying new audiences, increasing ticket sales and creating meaningful activity around the work.

Programmes can be created from scratch, built into Arts Council England applications, or developed around an existing funded or commercial project.

Trainee & Shadow Placements >

Paid Trainee Assistant Director, Trainee Assistant Producer and Shadow Director placements create practical pathways for emerging creatives to gain experience within professional rehearsal and production processes.

Placements are structured and mentored, with participants supported to move beyond observation and take on meaningful responsibilities within the rehearsal room or wider production.

Creative Workshops >

Simon programmes practical workshops for emerging artists across writing, directing, producing and theatre-making, led by established industry practitioners.

Previous facilitators include Rikki Beadle-Blair MBE, Tristan Fynn-Aiduenu and Somebody Jones.

Workshops can operate as standalone opportunities or form the first stage of a wider development pathway into paid placements, commissions or other opportunities.

Response Play Commissions >

Simon creates paid response-play programmes in which emerging writers develop short work inspired by the themes or world of a production.

Writers can receive dramaturgical support, collaborate with directors and see their work rehearsed and performed alongside the wider production.

These programmes can also create progression opportunities for Trainee Assistant Directors, giving them responsibility for directing new work with professional or emerging actors.

Audio & Digital Development >

Simon develops short-form audio drama and digital programmes combining workshops, mentoring and paid commissions.

Working with recording studios and audio practitioners, emerging writers can develop new work from initial idea through to recording and sharing, creating another route into professional creative practice beyond the stage.

Q&As & Post-Show Events >

Simon develops events around productions that create further opportunities for audiences and artists to meet and engage with the work.

These can include artist and specialist Q&As, facilitated conversations, workshops, queer nightlife events, focus groups and more, all tailored to the production and its audiences.

Bespoke Programme Design >

Bring Simon onto a project early and he can build the entire engagement strand around it. This can include:

  • designing activity for Arts Council England applications
  • building engagement budgets
  • creating trainee and artist-development programmes
  • recruiting and contracting participants
  • commissioning facilitators and artists
  • managing venues and community partners
  • delivering open calls and marketing campaigns
  • overseeing delivery and safeguarding
  • evaluating impact and gathering reporting evidence

From a single workshop or placement to a programme running throughout an entire production, activity is shaped around the work, its audiences and the artists it has the potential to support.

Developing a show? Let's build an engagement programme around it, support your funding & the next generation of artists.
