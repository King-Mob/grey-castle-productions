Positive

POSITIVE is a two-hander about Black, gay masculinity and HIV+ status, told through the relationship between two Black, gay best friends.

Recently single Malachi is hellbent on exploring his sexuality in any way he sees fit, whilst Ade is navigating life after recently finding out he is HIV positive.
